# Ethics

CANOPY is a research decision-support system. Recommendations are modeled scenarios, not autonomous policy directives.

- Population data are used only at aggregated grid-cell level.
- No personally identifiable information is collected or inferred.
- Socioeconomic analysis uses ward- or grid-level proxies only where data exist.
- Benefits and burdens of recommended interventions may distribute unequally; equity analysis is mandatory before policy use.
- Data gaps may bias recommendations toward well-observed areas; data-quality layers must accompany outputs.
- High-resolution imagery annotation must comply with provider terms of service.
