# Limitations

- Bengaluru is the sole primary study area; external validity is unproven until cross-city experiments run.
- 30 m Sentinel/Landsat resolution misses scattered roadside trees; results may not hold at crown scale.
- Monsoon cloud gaps can dominate detection delay; clear-sky and all-observation metrics must be reported separately.
- No public city-scale tree-removal ledger exists; ground truth relies on manual interpretation samples.
- LST is a proxy for human thermal stress; air-temperature validation is limited by sparse IMD stations.
- Intervention effect functions use configurable parameters; they are modeled scenarios, not observed causal impacts.
- WorldPop and ERA5-Land introduce spatial smoothing uncertainty.
- Optimization uses greedy heuristics, not guaranteed global optimum for multi-objective problems.
- Synthetic MVRE runs demonstrate pipeline wiring only; they are not scientific conclusions.
