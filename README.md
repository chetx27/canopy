# CANOPY

**Temporal Geospatial AI for Urban Vegetation Loss Detection & Climate Risk Mitigation**

---

## Overview

CANOPY is an end-to-end geospatial intelligence system for early detection of abnormal vegetation loss in urban environments, climate risk forecasting, and evidence-driven intervention optimization. Using satellite imagery and temporal analysis, CANOPY identifies vegetation stress patterns, projects future loss trajectories, quantifies heat exposure impacts, and recommends optimal tree-planting strategies within realistic operational constraints.

**Primary Application:** Bengaluru urban greenspace monitoring and resilience planning.

---

## Core Capabilities

- **Anomaly Detection** – Identifies abnormal vegetation change patterns against historical baselines using multispectral satellite data
- **Predictive Forecasting** – Projects future vegetation loss trajectories with uncertainty quantification
- **Impact Assessment** – Estimates heat exposure and urban heat island (UHI) mitigation potential
- **Intervention Optimization** – Recommends tree-planting locations balancing budget constraints, water availability, and land access
- **Baseline Comparison** – Benchmarks against historical and regional vegetation trends

## Tech Stack

- **Satellite Data:** Sentinel-2, Planet Labs, MODIS
- **Processing:** Python, GeoPandas, Rasterio, xarray
- **ML/Modeling:** PyTorch, scikit-learn, uncertainty quantification
- **Geospatial:** GDAL, PostGIS, Folium
- **Deployment:** Docker, cloud-based inference pipeline

---

## Key Outputs

- Vegetation anomaly maps with confidence intervals
- Risk stratification by region and time horizon
- Intervention ROI estimates and spatial priority layers
- Interactive dashboards for stakeholder decision-making
